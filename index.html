What MrSynker does

Sync your Spotify playlists to local MP3 files — perfect for filling a Walkman, a car USB stick, or anything that plays MP3s.

  • Read playlists and Liked Songs from your Spotify account
  • Find each track on YouTube via yt-dlp using ISRC matching
  • Convert to MP3 and write full ID3 tags (with cover art)
  • Track sync state per folder so updates are incremental

You'll need: yt-dlp and ffmpeg installed and on your PATH, plus a (free) Spotify Developer app.

Connect to Spotify

Create a free Spotify Developer app, copy your Client ID below, and add the redirect URI to your app settings.

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and click Create app.
  2. Add http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback to the app's Redirect URIs.
  3. Save, then copy the Client ID from the app page.
